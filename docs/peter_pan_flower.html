Blossom Topology Anatomy

Geometric Feature Mappings

Petal Length: Word Count

Longer petals indicate wordier chapters with extended structural prose sections.

Petal Width: Lexical Diversity

Broad, flared petals represent a highly varied, unique vocabulary profile within the chapter.

Petal Tip Curl: Sentiment Intensity

Upward curling tips represent narrative optimism, while downward folding tips signal somber text.

Edge Ripple: Punctuation Velocity

High-frequency edge waves reveal heavy use of commas, semicolons, and structural dialogue marks.
